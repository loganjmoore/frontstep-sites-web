Regular home care

A recurring-cleaning page could describe kitchen surfaces, bathrooms, floors, and the rooms the owner includes in a normal visit.

A more detailed reset

A deep-clean page could explain the extra attention available for edges, fixtures, and overlooked areas, with any exclusions stated clearly.

Moving in or moving on

A moving-clean page could explain whether the home needs to be empty, which cupboards or appliances are included, and what to prepare.

A useful conversation starts before the first clean.

This design leaves room for practical details instead of made-up reviews or credentials. These are example questions a cleaning-business owner might answer on their site.

  • Which rooms and surfaces are included?
  • What should be cleared before a visit?
  • Who provides the supplies?
  • How should pets or access instructions be handled?
