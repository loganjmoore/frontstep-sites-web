# Fictional cleaning design example

This route is an explicitly fictional static marketing demonstration, not an operating cleaning business or customer portfolio entry. It shows a different composition from the generated van/livery trade previews, which remain unchanged.

Use an editorial service menu, large Georgia headings, warm paper, wine-colored service band, open ruled service rows, practical preparation questions, and a local-only example brief. No real business identity, prices, claims, reviews, credentials, bookings or payments. The fictional-example notice stays prominent at every viewport. Preview inquiries point to the existing FrontStep mailbox; the footer returns to FrontStep. The homepage retains its own incumbent design.

Native semantic links, labels, keyboard focus, 48px controls, reduced-motion support, and responsive single-column flow are required. No paid images, generated raster assets, external fonts, customer data, or new dependencies. The example is noindex and intentionally absent from the article generator and sitemap. Demo inputs must never send, persist, or create a booking; text is echoed only through textContent.
