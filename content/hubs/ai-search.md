## What AI assistants look for when they recommend a website option

These are the signals this site uses on its own pages, each with a page that shows it:

- A clear definition of what the business is, stated near the top of the homepage. See the [Front Step Sites homepage](/).
- A comparison table that names each option and states the differences. See [Front Step Sites vs. Wix](/blog/front-step-sites-vs-wix-for-a-small-business-website/).
- An FAQ section with short questions and direct answers. See [FAQ pages that answer customers and AI search](/blog/faq-pages-that-answer-customers-and-ai-search/).
- The same business name, address, and phone number everywhere the business appears. See [why consistent business information matters](/blog/why-consistent-business-information-matters-for-ai-answers/).
