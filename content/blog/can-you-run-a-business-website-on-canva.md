Canva is where many owners already make flyers, menus and business cards, so building a website there may be a familiar next step. A useful business site still needs to help a customer understand your services and contact you. Test that journey before you commit to any builder.

## Canva Websites review: pros, cons, and verdict

**Pros:** Canva has a familiar drag-and-drop editor, supports one-page and multipage sites, and lets an owner reuse approved words, images, graphics, and video from their Canva work. It can fit a simple brochure site when the same person is willing to maintain it.

**Cons:** The owner still has to write and verify the service details, test the contact route, check domain and renewal arrangements, inspect each important page's title and description, and keep the published site current. A familiar design editor does not remove those business and maintenance jobs.

**Verdict:** Canva Websites works best for an owner who already likes Canva, needs a straightforward site, and can test the exact publishing and update workflow in the intended plan. A done-for-you service fits better when writing, building, and ongoing edits are work the owner wants to hand off.

| Plan and published price | Main pro | Main con | Review verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Canva Free ($0) | Publish up to five sites on a free my.canva.site address and test the editor without buying a website plan | Shows Canva branding, does not connect an existing domain, and publishes at most 10 sections per page | Useful for a prototype or temporary information site, not the final test of a business domain |
| Eligible paid Canva plan (price varies by plan, billing method, and region) | Connect an existing domain, publish unlimited sites on the free Canva domain, and publish up to 20 sections per page | The owner still handles the words, design, publishing, testing, and later updates | Consider it only after checking the current upgrade price and completing the same customer journey on the intended plan |

## Three example Canva websites to build and review

These are practical test plans, not customer sites or claims that Canva provides these businesses. Use the one closest to your needs and judge the finished draft by the listed customer task.

| Example Canva site | Pages or sections to build | Customer task to test | What would fail the review |
|---|---|---|---|
| Local plumber brochure | Home, drain cleaning, water heaters, service area, about, and contact | Find the right service, confirm coverage, and use the phone or enquiry route | Generic services, an invented location, or a contact route that is not tested end to end |
| Salon service site | Home, services and prices, team, gallery, policies, and contact | Check a current price, choose the right service, and contact the salon on a phone | Stale prices, decorative text that is hard to read, or navigation that hides the service list |
| One-off event site | Summary, date and location, schedule, accessibility details, registration link, and contact | Confirm when and where the event happens, then open the real registration route | Missing access information, an outdated schedule, or a registration link that loses the event context |

## Can Canva build a multipage business website?

Yes. Canva's current [official website builder page](https://www.canva.com/website-builder/) describes adding multiple pages and offers both one-page and multipage templates. The older advice that nine business pages require nine separate Canva websites is out of date.

For example, a plumber could plan a home page, five service pages, an about page, a service-area page and a contact page. Needing those nine pages is not, by itself, a reason to reject Canva. Build a small sample and check how visitors move between its pages.

## Canva Websites limits to check (verified October 24, 2026)

Canva's current help pages describe these publishing limits. Re-check Canva's help pages on the day you publish, because plan eligibility and limits can change.

| Limit | Canva Free | Eligible paid plans |
|---|---|---|
| Pages in one multipage website | Up to 45 pages | Up to 45 pages |
| Sites on the free my.canva.site domain | Up to five published sites | Unlimited published sites |
| Externally purchased domains connected to Canva | Not available | Up to five connected domains |
| Sections on each page | Design up to 20, but publish up to 10 | Publish up to 20 |

Connecting a domain bought outside Canva requires an eligible paid plan. The limits above describe Canva Websites, not mobile website or Link in Bio templates, which have different publishing options.

## What can you design in Canva?

Canva provides a drag-and-drop editor for text, images, graphics and video, with collaboration and website templates. Those are useful starting points for an owner who wants to do the design work personally. They do not replace writing clear service information or testing enquiries.

## What should you test before choosing a builder?

Use the same customer tasks to compare Canva with another builder or a done-for-you service:

| Customer or owner task | What to check in a sample site |
|---|---|
| Find the right service | Open each service page from the navigation and return to the home page. |
| Call or request an estimate | Use the phone link and submit a test enquiry; confirm it reaches the right inbox. |
| Use your business address online | Check domain connection, renewal costs and who controls the registration. |
| Understand a page from search | Inspect the published page title and description for each important page. |
| Update business details | Change a sample opening time, photo and service description yourself. |
| Book or pay, if needed | Test your chosen booking or payment integration and check its separate fees. |

These are checks to perform, not a claim that Canva lacks those features. Verify the options and limits in the specific plan and integrations you intend to use. For a blog or catalogue, create a sample entry and test navigation and later updates before moving your existing content.

## What should you check about hosting and domains?

Confirm your publishing address, custom-domain options, ongoing costs and account ownership before launch. Keep a record of the domain registrar and renewal date. ## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use Canva for a business website at all?

Yes. It supports multipage websites as well as one-page sites. Test whether your chosen plan and integrations meet your contact, publishing and maintenance needs.

### Do I need a domain name for a Canva website?

If you want a business address you control, check the current custom-domain publishing options and renewal costs. Keep control of the registration and the account that manages it.

### Is Canva Websites free, and what are the current limits?

Canva Free can publish up to five sites on the free my.canva.site domain, with as many as 45 pages per multipage site and up to 10 published sections per page. Eligible paid plans can publish unlimited sites on that free domain, publish up to 20 sections per page, and connect up to five externally purchased domains. Re-check Canva's help pages before publishing because these limits can change.

### Can I start on Canva and move to a different builder later?

Before starting, ask the destination provider what content and design can be transferred and what must be rebuilt. Keep copies of your text and original images, and retain control of your domain so you can change where it points.

## Before you decide

Canva's multipage support removes the old one-page-only objection. Compare the actual enquiry journey, publishing controls, ongoing costs and who will maintain your site. A working sample will tell you more than an outdated feature comparison.
