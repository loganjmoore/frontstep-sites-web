To build a website with Wix, gather your business facts first, choose a template or AI starting point, create the essential pages, connect a business domain, and test every customer action before publishing. This 11-point checklist takes you from an editable Wix draft to a business site that can be used on a phone and found through your Google Business Profile. Complete the checks in order because a polished template can still hide missing information.

## What should you prepare before opening the Wix editor?

A Wix website build should begin with one customer goal and a small packet of verified business facts. Decide whether the main action is a call, quote request, booking, purchase, or visit, then collect the information that supports it.

Prepare these items:

1. The business name exactly as customers see it elsewhere.
2. One main phone number or monitored email address.
3. Current hours and a real service area or street address.
4. A short list of the services you want customers to request.
5. Original photos you have permission to publish.
6. Any license, insurance, pricing, or policy claims you can verify.

Keep the domain and Wix account under a business-controlled login. A friend or contractor can be invited to help, but the owner should retain the credentials needed to renew the domain, change billing, and remove access later. If the difference between a domain, hosting, pages, and content still feels blurry, [the four-part website guide](/blog/what-is-a-website-four-parts/) explains what each piece does.

## Which Wix starting point should you choose?

The Wix starting point should match how much control you want during the build. Wix offers an AI-led setup and a template-based editor; both still require you to supply accurate business details, choose the useful pages, and approve the finished copy.

Wix Help Center describes its setup as a sequence that includes choosing a builder, designing the site, customizing content, completing its SEO checklist, reviewing, and publishing. Treat the generated design as a draft. Remove sample services, invented testimonials, stock contact details, and sections that do not help your customer take the main action.

Choose a template whose structure fits the job. The resemblance between its demo business and yours is secondary. A roofer may need project photos and an estimate path. A restaurant needs hours, location, and a readable menu. The colors in either template can change; the information path matters more.

## Which pages does a small business Wix site need?

A small business Wix site usually needs enough pages to answer separate customer questions without making the navigation hard to scan. Start with Home, Services, About, and Contact, then add a page only when it has a distinct job.

| Page | Question it should answer | Main proof or action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Am I in the right place? | Service, location, and primary action |
| Services | Can this business handle my need? | Specific work and boundaries |
| About | Who will do the work? | Real people, experience, or process |
| Contact | How do I start? | Working call, form, booking, or directions |

Use that four-page plan as a baseline. A one-page site may be enough for a narrow offer, while a business with several high-value services may need separate pages. Give each page one clear subject and one useful next step.

## What should the first screen say?

The first screen of a Wix business website should name the service, the place served, and the next action in plain language. A visitor should understand those facts before scrolling through awards, company history, or a gallery.

Use a sentence such as “Residential plumbing repair in Madison. Call to request a weekday appointment.” Then support it with a real photograph and a button that repeats the action. Avoid claims such as “best,” “number one,” or “available 24/7” unless the business can substantiate them.

Write page titles and descriptions for humans as well as search results. A title such as “Water heater repair in Madison | Acme Plumbing” carries more information than “Services.” The [three-question website-builder scorecard](/blog/best-website-builder-three-questions/) can help you check control and upkeep before you invest more time in the editor.

## How should you check the Wix site on a phone?

The Wix phone check should confirm that every important fact and action survives the smaller screen. [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/mobile/mobile-sites-mobile-first-indexing) says Google uses the mobile version of a site for indexing and ranking, and it recommends responsive web design because the same URL and HTML can adapt to different screen sizes.

Open Wix's mobile preview, then test the published site on a real phone. Read the heading without zooming. Open the menu with one hand. Tap the phone number, submit the form, inspect image crops, and make sure no banner covers the main button. Keep the same essential content and descriptive headings on both layouts.

A desktop preview cannot test the phone dialer, a slow cellular connection, or the way a real thumb reaches controls. The guide to [building business websites for phones first](/blog/why-your-small-business-website-should-be-built-for-phones-first/) covers that customer journey in more detail.

## What domain and search settings should you check?

The Wix domain and search checks should confirm that the public address belongs to the business and each page has a useful search preview. Wix Help Center says a free Wix URL uses the format `siteprefix.wixsite.com/siteaddress`; connecting a custom domain requires a paid site plan.

Before launch, confirm all of the following:

- The intended custom domain opens the right site over HTTPS.
- The domain renews in an account the business controls.
- Every important page has a distinct title and description.
- Search engines are allowed to index the live pages.
- The social-share image, business name, and favicon are intentional.

Wix says its free hosting includes 500 MB of storage and 500 MB of bandwidth. Those numbers may be enough for a draft, but the free Wix address is a separate decision from the storage allowance. Check the current plan and checkout total before relying on any price or feature.

## How should the Wix site connect to Google Business Profile?

The Wix site should use the same core business facts as the verified Google Business Profile and the profile should link to the final business domain. [Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/2911778) says adding and verifying a profile lets customers find the business on Google Search and Maps at no charge.

Compare the name, phone, hours, address or service area, and website URL side by side. Fix genuine differences instead of forcing wording to match when the two surfaces serve different purposes. The profile can help a customer discover the business; the website can explain services, policies, photos, and the next step in more depth.

## What is the 11-point Wix launch test?

The 11-point Wix launch test is a scored walkthrough of the real published site, with one point for each completed check. Run it while signed out so editor access does not conceal a public problem.

| Check | Pass condition |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Account | Business controls login and recovery |
| 2. Goal | One primary customer action is obvious |
| 3. Facts | Name, phone, hours, and area are current |
| 4. Pages | Each navigation item has a distinct job |
| 5. Copy | No sample or unverified text remains |
| 6. Phone | No zooming, clipping, or blocked controls |
| 7. Actions | Calls, forms, bookings, and directions work |
| 8. Domain | The secure custom address resolves correctly |
| 9. Search | Titles, descriptions, and indexing are set |
| 10. Profile | Google Business Profile uses the final URL |
| 11. Public test | A signed-out visitor can finish the task |

A score of 11 means the defined checks passed. Rankings and future edits sit outside this test. Record the date, tester, and failed item. Repair every failed customer action before putting the URL on a truck, menu, sign, or invoice.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I build a Wix website for free?

Yes. Wix lets you build and publish with a Wix-branded address, and its Help Center says free hosting includes 500 MB of storage and 500 MB of bandwidth. A custom business domain requires an eligible paid plan, so check the current checkout before launch.

### Does a Google Business Profile replace a Wix website?

No. A Google Business Profile helps eligible businesses appear on Search and Maps, while a Wix website can hold fuller service information and a customer path the business controls. Use consistent facts and link the profile to the final site.

### How many pages should a Wix business site have?

Start with the fewest pages that answer distinct customer questions. Home, Services, About, and Contact are a useful four-page baseline, but a narrow business may need less and a multi-service business may need more.

### Should I publish before connecting the custom domain?

You can publish a draft to its Wix address for testing. Connect and test the custom domain before advertising it so customers, listings, analytics, and printed materials use one stable address.
