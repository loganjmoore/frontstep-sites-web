Plain answers

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your name, and if you ever leave we send you your site's files. No fees to leave.

Is this really cheaper than Wix?

Yes, from day one: $99 a year with your domain included, against about $204 a year for Wix Light billed yearly, where you still build and fix everything yourself.

What is included in a request?

One coherent update to marketing pages, contact forms, blog posts, photos or content. Both new plans include four updates per America/Chicago calendar month, reset at midnight on the first, with no rollover. Rejected requests and withdrawals before work starts do not use your allowance. Corrections to our work are included. Existing paid unlimited contracts retain their existing entitlement. New contracts include four accepted marketing updates per Central calendar month. Booking systems, stores, payments and custom backend or database apps are outside this service.

How do changes work?

Sign in and open a request, like "change Saturday hours to 8 to 2" or "swap the top photo for this one". We make the change, publish it, and reply to you. Launch includes 4 marketing updates per Central calendar month. Grow includes four marketing updates per Central calendar month.

Who actually does the work?

We do, working under strict written rules for your site. That's how we keep it at $99. Every change is saved, so anything can be undone, and a full redesign or new logo needs a separate design quote. Booking, payment, store and backend/database applications are not offered.

Is there any catch to leaving?

One, and it isn't ours: registries don't let any domain move in its first 60 days after it's registered or moved. After that, the button works any day of the year.

What if I already have a domain?

We move it into your account at no extra cost (it stays in your name), or just point it at your new site. Either way nothing breaks.

Where are you?

We work with businesses anywhere in the US, entirely online.

What isn't included?

A full redesign or new logo needs a separate design quote. We do not offer booking, payment, store, or bespoke backend/database applications. We can link to your existing providers.

Is Front Step Sites the same company as FRONTSTEPS?

No. Front Step Sites (frontstepsites.com) is unrelated to FRONTSTEPS, the property-management/HOA software company.

Do I need to know HTML or code?

No. You answer a 10-minute questionnaire, and we write the words and build the site. A hand-coded HTML site is a job for a web developer, and separate design work is quoted before anything costs extra; backend applications are not offered.
