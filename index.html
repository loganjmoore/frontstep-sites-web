Plain answers

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your name, and if you ever leave we send you your site's files. No fees to leave.

Is this really cheaper than Wix?

Not in year one: $249 setup plus $99 is $348, against about $204 to build it yourself on Wix. The setup pays a real person to write and build the whole site so you don't spend your evenings on it. Every year after, you pay $99 plus your domain, and Wix is still $204 or more, with you doing the work.

How do changes work?

Email what you want changed. On Launch, one content refresh a year is included. On Grow, small changes are unlimited and live within two business days.

Is there any catch to leaving?

One, and it isn't ours: registries don't let any domain move in its first 60 days after it's registered or moved. After that, the button works any day of the year.

What if I already have a domain?

We can move it into your account (it stays in your name) or just point it at your new site. Either way nothing breaks.

Where are you?

We work with businesses anywhere in the US, by email and a scheduled call.

What isn't included?

Online stores, booking systems, payments, and custom features. We'll quote those before anything costs extra.
