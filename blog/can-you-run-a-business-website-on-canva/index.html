Can you run a business website on Canva?

Canva Websites is a page builder inside Canva with one-page and multipage website templates.

Updated October 27, 2026

Short answer

Yes. Canva supports multipage websites, so needing service, about and contact pages does not rule it out. Before choosing it, test your domain, customer enquiry path and ongoing updates in the plan you intend to use. The decision is whether you want to maintain the site yourself or pay a service to do that work.

Canva is where many owners already make flyers, menus and business cards, so building a website there may be a familiar next step. A useful business site still needs to help a customer understand your services and contact you. Test that journey before you commit to any builder.

Canva Websites review: pros, cons, and verdict

Pros: Canva has a familiar drag-and-drop editor, supports one-page and multipage sites, and lets an owner reuse approved words, images, graphics, and video from their Canva work. It can fit a simple brochure site when the same person is willing to maintain it.

Cons: The owner still has to write and verify the service details, test the contact route, check domain and renewal arrangements, inspect each important page's title and description, and keep the published site current. A familiar design editor does not remove those business and maintenance jobs.

Verdict: Canva Websites works best for an owner who already likes Canva, needs a straightforward site, and can test the exact publishing and update workflow in the intended plan. A done-for-you service fits better when writing, building, and ongoing edits are work the owner wants to hand off.

Can Canva build a multipage business website?

Yes. Canva's current official website builder page describes adding multiple pages and offers both one-page and multipage templates. The older advice that nine business pages require nine separate Canva websites is out of date.

For example, a plumber could plan a home page, five service pages, an about page, a service-area page and a contact page. Needing those nine pages is not, by itself, a reason to reject Canva. Build a small sample and check how visitors move between its pages.

What can you design in Canva?

Canva provides a drag-and-drop editor for text, images, graphics and video, with collaboration and website templates. Those are useful starting points for an owner who wants to do the design work personally. They do not replace writing clear service information or testing enquiries.

What should you check about hosting and domains?

Confirm your publishing address, custom-domain options, ongoing costs and account ownership before launch. Keep a record of the domain registrar and renewal date. The plain-English guide to website hosting explains hosting, and the domain name guide helps you choose an address.

When is a done-for-you service a better fit?

Choose based on who will write, build and maintain the site. If you enjoy editing in Canva and can keep the information current, try that workflow. If you would rather send updates to someone else, compare the scope and ongoing cost of a managed service.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Canva for a business website at all?

Yes. It supports multipage websites as well as one-page sites. Test whether your chosen plan and integrations meet your contact, publishing and maintenance needs.

Do I need a domain name for a Canva website?

If you want a business address you control, check the current custom-domain publishing options and renewal costs. Keep control of the registration and the account that manages it.

Is Canva Websites free, and what are the current limits?

Canva Free can publish up to five sites on the free my.canva.site domain, with as many as 45 pages per multipage site and up to 10 published sections per page. Eligible paid plans can publish unlimited sites on that free domain, publish up to 20 sections per page, and connect up to five externally purchased domains. Re-check Canva's help pages before publishing because these limits can change.

Can I start on Canva and move to a different builder later?

Before starting, ask the destination provider what content and design can be transferred and what must be rebuilt. Keep copies of your text and original images, and retain control of your domain so you can change where it points.

Before you decide

Canva's multipage support removes the old one-page-only objection. Compare the actual enquiry journey, publishing controls, ongoing costs and who will maintain your site. A working sample will tell you more than an outdated feature comparison.
