  • How AI search is changing local search

    AI search gives customers another way to compare local businesses and ask follow-up questions before calling. Your practical priorities are clear service pages, accurate business details, and a working contact process. No special file or wording guarantees a recommendation, so judge your efforts by accurate information and suitable customer inquiries.

  • How to show up when someone asks ChatGPT

    Start with accurate, accessible information about your services, service area, hours, and contact details. Keep your business listings consistent, collect genuine reviews, and ask your website provider to check search access and structured data. These steps make your business easier to understand and verify, but they do not guarantee that ChatGPT will recommend you.

  • Google AI Overviews: what to do now

    Start with clear service pages, accurate business information, and answers to the questions customers ask before hiring you. Google says its normal search fundamentals still apply to AI Overviews, with no special optimization required. These steps give customers useful information, but they do not guarantee that Google will mention or link to your business.

  • What is GEO for small businesses?

    GEO means generative engine optimization: improving the information about your business so AI search tools can understand it and potentially use it in answers. For a local business, the useful work overlaps with ordinary SEO: accurate service details, clear answers, accessible pages, and consistent listings. None of it guarantees a recommendation.

  • Writing website content AI can quote

    Write website content AI assistants can quote by answering a real customer question first, then adding specific facts and clear conditions. Use descriptive headings, readable text, and accurate business details. This makes your pages easier to understand and check, but no writing format can guarantee that an assistant will quote or recommend your business.

  • Structured data for AI search basics

    Structured data labels facts on your website so software can interpret them more easily. A small business should start with accurate business details that match its visible pages, then check the result. There is no special AI schema that guarantees recommendations, and review or FAQ markup does not automatically produce extra search features.

  • Consistent business info matters for AI

    Consistent business information gives customers and systems checking public sources fewer conflicting facts to sort through. Keep your business name, phone, location or service area, hours, and services current wherever you publish them. This reduces avoidable confusion, but it does not guarantee accurate AI answers or prove that every assistant uses the same sources.

  • How reviews influence AI recommendations

    Reviews can supply information that AI tools use when describing or recommending local businesses, but access and selection differ by tool. There is no universal review count or star rating that guarantees inclusion. Ask customers for honest feedback, leave the wording to them, and keep your own service information accurate.

  • Voice search for local businesses

    For local voice search, focus on accurate business listings, clear service descriptions, current hours, and a working phone number. Answer the questions someone would ask aloud, such as whether you handle their problem or serve their town. No special wording guarantees a recommendation, so check the facts customers receive and keep the underlying sources current.

  • What llms.txt is and if you need one

    An llms.txt file is an optional guide that points AI tools toward useful information on a website. It is a proposal, not a requirement for a local business website. Add one if it is simple to maintain and has a clear purpose, but fix missing services, incorrect business details, and broken contact forms first.

  • Check what AI assistants say about you

    Ask a short, repeatable set of customer questions, save the answers and source links, and compare each business fact with your current records. Fix incorrect source pages first, then check again. Treat a missing recommendation differently from a wrong phone number, and do not assume one conversation represents what every customer sees.

  • FAQ pages for customers and AI search

    A useful FAQ answers the questions customers ask before booking, with the direct answer first and any limits explained immediately after it. Put service questions on the relevant service page and shared policies on a central FAQ. Clear answers may inform AI search, but formatting and schema do not guarantee inclusion.
